Pakistan Atlas — trade routes

An interactive 2D map for Pakistan Studies. Pan and zoom a regional map of Pakistan, neighbouring countries, the Arabian Sea, the Gulf of Oman, the Strait of Hormuz and the Persian Gulf. The China–Pakistan Economic Corridor is drawn as a schematic through documented places from Kashgar to Gwadar. Maritime lines are illustrative corridors, not ship tracks. Tap a port, route or label for notes. Country outlines are Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain). Provincial boundaries and the Line of Control come from this atlas’s existing geographic data.
